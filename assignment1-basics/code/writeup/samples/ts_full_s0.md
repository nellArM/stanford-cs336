Once upon a time, there was a big, bossy cat named Tom. Tom liked to play in the sun all day. He had a lot of fun. One day, Tom saw a little bug. The bug was very small and wanted to escape.
Tom thought of a plan. He would find a way to catch the bug. He saw a big box and thought it would be a good place to hide. Tom hid behind the box and waited for the bug to come close.
But, the bug did not come. Instead, it jumped out and ran away. Tom tried to catch the bug, but it was too fast. He did not catch the bug. Tom was sad because he could not catch the bug. He went home and told his mom about the bug.
